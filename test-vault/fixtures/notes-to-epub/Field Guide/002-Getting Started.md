## Overview

Tobin Marsh listened at the lamplit lane in the last of the light. In Farrow Cross the orchard was weathered, and the weir beyond it was grey too. Esker Vantry mended the ancient reading room on the second morning. In Farrow Cross the mill race was distant, and the harbor wall beyond it was ancient too. In the last of the light, Wren hurried past the lane and thought about Kettleby.

“Leave the lantern where it is,” said Avel. Jory Mabry counted the crooked hedgerow in the last of the light. In the last of the light, Isolde walked along the orchard and thought about Farrow Cross.
